    1099 tax questions

    How is tax on 1099 income calculated?

    You pay two federal taxes. Self-employment tax is 15.3% of 92.35% of your net profit and covers Social Security and Medicare. Federal income tax applies to your total income after half of the self-employment tax, the standard deduction and the 20% business income deduction. Your net profit is your 1099 income minus business expenses, so every deductible expense lowers both taxes.

    How much of my 1099 income should I set aside?

    Most freelancers set aside 20% to 30% of each payment for federal tax, plus whatever their state charges. The calculator shows the share that matches your own income and expenses under “Set aside from each 1099 payment”.

    When are 2026 quarterly estimated taxes due?

    The due dates for 2026 are April 15, June 15 and September 15, 2026, and January 15, 2027. You generally need to make estimated payments if you expect to owe $1,000 or more after withholding.

    How do I avoid an underpayment penalty?

    You usually avoid the penalty if your withholding and estimated payments add up to at least 90% of this year’s tax or 100% of last year’s tax, whichever is smaller. If your adjusted gross income last year was over $150,000, the last-year figure is 110%. Enter your 2025 tax in the calculator to see your minimum.

    Can I deduct my home office?

    Yes, if you use part of your home regularly and only for your business. The simplified method lets you deduct $5 per square foot for up to 300 square feet, which is what the calculator uses. You can’t use it to create a loss.

    Is all of my 1099-K income taxable?

    Only the business part. A 1099-K reports payments you received through apps and marketplaces, which can include personal transfers or items you sold at a loss. Enter only the amount that was income from your work.
