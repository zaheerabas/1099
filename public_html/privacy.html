Privacy

The short version: the numbers you type into the calculator stay in your browser.

Last updated October 26, 2026

What happens to your numbers

The 1099 calculator runs entirely in your browser. Your income, expenses and other answers are never sent to us or to anyone else.

So your answers are still there next time, the calculator saves them in your browser’s local storage on your own device. Select “Clear all” in the calculator to delete them, or clear your browser’s site data.

If you use “Copy link”, your answers are written into the link itself. Anyone you share that link with can see those numbers.

Services this site uses

  • Hosting: like any website, our host keeps standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to run and protect the site.
  • Google Fonts: the typeface loads from Google, which receives your IP address when it’s requested. See Google’s privacy policy.

We don’t use advertising cookies, and we don’t sell or share personal information.
