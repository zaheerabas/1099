The basics

What is a 1099 form?

A 1099 is an IRS form that reports money you received that wasn’t a salary. If you freelance, contract or sell through apps, clients and platforms send you a 1099 instead of a W-2, and nothing is withheld for tax. You report that income and pay the tax yourself.

What’s the difference between a 1099-NEC, 1099-K and 1099-MISC?
  • 1099-NEC: pay from a client for your work, such as freelance or contract fees.
  • 1099-K: payments that came through a payment app, card processor or online marketplace.
  • 1099-MISC: other income, such as rents, prizes or some royalties.

All three can be business income. The calculator has a box for each.

Do I owe tax if I didn’t get a 1099?

Yes. You owe tax on all your business income, whether or not anyone sends you a form. Keep your own record of every payment, especially from smaller clients.

When does a client have to send me a 1099-NEC?

For payments made in 2026, a client sends a 1099-NEC once they’ve paid you $2,000 or more in the year. Before 2026 the threshold was $600. The form is due to you by January 31; for 2026 payments that’s February 1, 2027, because January 31 falls on a Sunday.

Is everything on my 1099-K taxable?

Only the business part. A 1099-K can include personal transfers, refunds or items you sold for less than you paid. Payment apps send one once you pass $20,000 and 200 transactions in a year. Enter only the income from your work.

Self-employment tax

What is self-employment tax?

It’s Social Security and Medicare tax for people who work for themselves. An employer normally pays half of it for employees; when you’re self-employed you pay both halves. It’s 15.3% of 92.35% of your net profit: 12.4% for Social Security and 2.9% for Medicare.

Is there a limit on self-employment tax?

The 12.4% Social Security part stops at the 2026 wage base of $184,500, counting any W-2 wages first. The 2.9% Medicare part has no limit, and an extra 0.9% applies above $200,000 of earnings ($250,000 married filing jointly).

Do I pay self-employment tax on small amounts?

If your net earnings from self-employment are under $400 for the year, there’s no self-employment tax. You may still owe income tax on that money.

Can I deduct my self-employment tax?

Half of it. Half of your self-employment tax is deducted from your income, which lowers your income tax. The calculator does this for you.

Deductions

Can I deduct my home office?

Yes, if you use part of your home regularly and only for your business. The simplified method gives $5 per square foot, up to 300 square feet ($1,500). It can’t create a loss. The regular method uses your actual home costs and takes more records.

How do I deduct driving for work?

Multiply your business miles by the IRS rate: 72.5¢ a mile from January to June 2026 and 76¢ from July 1. Driving between clients counts; commuting to a regular workplace doesn’t. Keep a log with the date, purpose and miles.

Can I deduct health insurance?

If you pay for your own health insurance and can’t join an employer’s plan (including a spouse’s), the premiums are usually deductible from your income, up to your business profit.

What is the 20% business income deduction?

The qualified business income deduction lets many self-employed people deduct up to 20% of their business income, limited to 20% of taxable income. From 2026 there’s a $400 minimum if your business income is at least $1,000. Extra limits start above $201,750 of taxable income ($403,500 joint).

Can I deduct my phone and internet?

The business share. If you use your phone for work 40% of the time, you can deduct 40% of the bill. Keep a note of how you worked out the share.

Do retirement contributions lower my tax?

Yes. Contributions to a SEP-IRA or solo 401(k) lower your income tax (not your self-employment tax). The calculator has a field for them under Payments and savings.

Quarterly payments

How much of my 1099 income should I set aside?

Many freelancers set aside 20% to 30% of each payment for federal tax, plus whatever their state charges. Your own share depends on your income, expenses and filing status. The calculator shows it under “Set aside from each 1099 payment”.

Do I have to pay quarterly estimated taxes?

Generally yes, if you expect to owe $1,000 or more for the year after withholding and credits. Freelancers with no withholding usually reach that quickly.

When are the 2026 estimated tax payments due?

April 15, June 15 and September 15, 2026, and January 15, 2027. The calculator shows how much to pay on each date that’s still ahead.

How do I make an estimated payment?

Online through IRS Direct Pay, your IRS Online Account or EFTPS, or by mailing a check with a Form 1040-ES voucher. Choose “estimated tax” and the right tax year, and keep the confirmation.

How do I avoid an underpayment penalty?

Pay at least the smaller of 90% of this year’s tax or 100% of last year’s tax through withholding and estimated payments. If your adjusted gross income last year was over $150,000 ($75,000 married filing separately), use 110% of last year’s tax. Enter your 2025 tax in the calculator to see your minimum.

What if I missed a payment?

Pay as soon as you can. The penalty works like interest: it’s worked out for each quarter and grows the longer the amount stays unpaid, so paying late is still better than waiting for April.

Do I have to pay state estimated taxes too?

Many states with an income tax also expect quarterly payments, with their own dates and rules. The taxmetter calculator covers federal tax only, so check your state’s tax website as well.

The calculator and your data

How accurate is the calculator?

It uses the 2026 IRS brackets, standard deduction, self-employment tax rules and mileage rates, and shows every step under “How we calculated this”. It doesn’t include state tax, credits, itemized deductions or capital gains, so treat the result as a planning estimate.

Do you store the numbers I enter?

No. The calculator runs in your browser and your numbers are never sent to us. They’re saved on your own device so they’re there when you return; “Clear all” deletes them. See Privacy.

Can I share my estimate with my accountant?

Yes. “Copy link” makes a link that opens the calculator with your numbers filled in, and “Print or save PDF” prints just the result. Anyone with the link can see those numbers.
