New 1099 thresholds for 2026: what changed and what didn’t

Clients now send a 1099-NEC at $2,000 instead of $600, and the 1099-K is back to $20,000. But one thing hasn’t changed: all your income is still taxable.

The amounts that make a business send you a 1099 form have changed for 2026. It’s good news for paperwork, but it’s easy to misread.

FormWhat it reports2026 threshold
1099-NECPay for your work as a contractor$2,000 from one payer (was $600)
1099-MISCRents, prizes and other income$2,000 for most payments
1099-KPayments through apps, card processors and marketplacesOver $20,000 and over 200 transactions

1099-NEC: now $2,000

Clients send a Form 1099-NEC when they pay you for your work as a contractor. From 2026 they only have to send one if they pay you $2,000 or more in the year. It used to be $600.

1099-MISC: also $2,000 for most payments

The 1099-MISC, used for rents, prizes and some other payments, moves to $2,000 for most types of payment too.

1099-K: back to $20,000

Payment apps, card processors and online marketplaces send a Form 1099-K. The threshold is over $20,000 and over 200 transactions in the year, after plans to lower it were reversed.

The part that didn’t change

Remember: no form doesn’t mean no tax. Every dollar you earn is taxable income.

These thresholds only decide whether a form is sent. They don’t change what’s taxable. If a client pays you $1,500, you won’t get a 1099-NEC, but the $1,500 is still income you must report.

In practice this means more freelancers will have income with no form behind it. Your own records matter more than ever.

Tips for tracking income without forms

  • Use one bank account for all business income, so every payment is in one place.
  • Keep a simple income log: date, client and amount.
  • At year end, compare the forms you received with your own total. The forms will usually add up to less.

When you get a 1099-K for personal payments

If a 1099-K includes money that isn’t business income, like friends paying you back for dinner, keep records showing what those payments were. You only pay tax on actual income.
